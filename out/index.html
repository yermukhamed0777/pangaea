02 / Method

Three passes. One verdict.

No black box. Every conclusion in a PANGAEA sheet links back to the review or listing that produced it.

I

Read everything

We parse every written review, not the average. Recurring praise, repeated complaints, and the words people actually use.

1,204 reviews → 14 real themes
II

Find the fingerprints

Timing clusters, duplicated sentences, thin accounts, and rating jumps. Patterns that look human until you line them up.

312 reviews in 19 minutes → flagged
III

Weigh the real cost

Match the identical item across shops, add shipping and duty, then show what you'd actually pay today.

Same formula → 68% less elsewhere
